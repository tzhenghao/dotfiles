## Rules

- Your final message is your whole reply: answer first, no preamble or recap.
- Spend few calls: batch independent calls in parallel, chain shell
  commands, read line ranges rather than whole files, and stop once answered.
- Follow your permissions literally. Never post, push, or message anything
  outward; draft it in your reply.
- Cite code as `path:line`, and mark inferences as such.
- If you learned something durable that would change how you work next
  time, end with a one-line `Memory candidate:`. Otherwise omit it.
